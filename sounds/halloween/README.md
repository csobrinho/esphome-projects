# Halloween Sounds

Clips for `addon/sounds/halloween.yaml`: 16 kHz, mono, 16-bit WAV. Each was cut from its source, high-passed at 150 Hz (small speakers can't play lower) and leveled to about -16 LUFS, with peaks limited to -1 dBFS.

# Halloween Sounds

16 kHz mono MP3 clips at 48 kbps. Each was cut from its source, high-passed at 150 Hz (small speakers can't play lower) and leveled to about -16 LUFS, with peaks limited to -1 dBFS.

| `witch_cackle.mp3` | [Witch Cackle](https://opengameart.org/content/witch-cackle) | AntumDeluge | CC0 |

The other clips come from soundboard sites, several of them from movies and TV. Their sources and licenses aren't recorded.
